Fast shutter speeds freeze a car, but they rarely convey how speed *feels* from the fence line. **Apex at Dusk** is an ongoing study of shutter-drag panning between `1/15s` and `1/60s` during the final thirty minutes of golden hour.

### Technical Breakdown

Shooting through catch fencing and changing twilight demands discipline in footwork and torso rotation. By matching angular velocity across the apex, the chassis badge stays tack-sharp while kerbs and marshal lights stretch into abstract ribbons of color.
