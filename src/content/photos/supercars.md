Cities transform when the pavement turns into a second sky. During three consecutive rainy nights in Tokyo, I walked with a single 35mm prime lens, looking for pockets of stillness inside the sensory overload of Shinjuku and Kabukicho.

### Field Notes & Approach

Rather than stopping crowds at peak hour, this series focuses on the transitional window between 11:30 PM and 1:30 AM. Umbrellas diffuse harsh signage into soft directional key lights, while puddles double the warm amber and cyan palette.

- **Lighting:** Ambient neon and storefront tungsten only; exposed for highlight preservation (-0.7 EV).
- **Color Grade:** Subtle split-toning with cool teal shadows and warm halation in the mid-tones.
